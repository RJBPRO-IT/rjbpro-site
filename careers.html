Life at RJB

The people behind the projects

Every project we deliver comes down to the people on it. Our field and office teams work side by side, from the first site survey to the final punch walk. When something comes up, nobody figures it out alone.

We want people to grow here. That means learning from experienced teammates, building new skills on real projects, and taking on more as you're ready.

RJB has been family-owned since 1992. It shows in how we work together: straight talk, shared accountability, and a team that has your back.
