Sheetz Survey Program

Overview

Sheetz came to RJB with a problem, not a scope. They needed accurate, consistent documentation of existing conditions across their entire store footprint, and they needed it fast enough to be useful for planning.

RJB built the solution around it. Using our own fleet and field teams, we surveyed more than 700 Sheetz locations in three months.

How we delivered

Every site was documented with 360-degree capture, so each location was recorded the same way. That consistency is what makes a survey program useful — it lets an owner compare stores against each other instead of reading 700 one-off reports.

The data went directly to Sheetz's servers. No repackaging, no waiting on a handoff. Their team could work with the information as it came in.

Covering that many locations on that schedule came down to routing and crew coordination. Our teams worked across the Mideast footprint on a planned sequence, keeping a steady pace through the program rather than surging and stalling.
